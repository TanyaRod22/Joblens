Joblens Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Joblens is a Chrome extension that helps you analyze job postings and prepare interview talking points. This policy explains what data the extension collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (name, headline, summary, skills, experience, resume text, and preferences) is saved locally in your browser using Chrome storage. Per-job tailored resume drafts and accepted/rejected suggestion choices are also stored locally. This data stays on your device until you delete your profile, clear site data, or uninstall the extension. Deleting your profile from the Profile tab removes your master profile and tailored drafts.

Data sent to our servers

When you scan a job or use AI features, Joblens may send the following to our backend API:

  • Job posting text scraped from the page you are viewing (title, company, location, description, URL)
  • Your profile information, if you have enabled “Include my profile in analysis”
  • Interview Prep chat messages you send while using the Prep tab
  • Resume files you upload for parsing (PDF, DOCX, or TXT)

Our backend processes this data and sends relevant content to OpenAI to generate analysis, fit scores, resume suggestions, interview answers, cold emails, and cover letters. We do not sell your data.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or collect your email address through the extension
  • We do not track your browsing history beyond the job page you choose to scan
  • We do not store your profile or resume on our servers after a request completes

Your choices

  • Turn off “Include my profile in analysis” in the Profile tab to run generic job analysis only
  • Delete your saved profile and tailored drafts at any time from the Profile tab
  • Uninstall the extension to remove locally stored data

Third-party services

  • Railway — hosts our API
  • OpenAI — powers AI analysis and text generation
